Who We Are

Dunedin's Trusted
Shoe Repair Experts

Country Cobbler has been restoring footwear across Dunedin for over two decades. We take pride in giving quality shoes and boots a second life — saving you money and keeping great footwear out of landfill.

✦
Expert Craftsmanship Every job is handled with care, skill, and attention to detail from our experienced team.
✦
All Footwear Welcome From dress shoes and heels to work boots and sneakers — we repair it all.
